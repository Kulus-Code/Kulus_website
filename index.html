About Kulus

Kulus is the younger brother of the thunderbird, a protective spirit known for learning and taking care of others. At Kulus Health, we embody this spirit by building a community-driven healthcare platform.

We are focused on the future of health, starting with diabetes and pre-diabetes in Indigenous communities. We believe in a model built on:

  • Data Sovereignty: You own and control your health data.
  • Holistic Health: Connecting modern care with traditional diets.
  • Accessibility: Promoting fitness and well-being at every age.

You Are the Owner, Not the Product.

We are imagining a world where:

  • Health platforms use open, secure, and accessible standards.
  • Patients are data owners and customers, not data servants.
  • You have the power and choice to monetize your own health data, if you wish.
  • Security and privacy are genuinely empowering you, not an excuse for data mining.

The Future of Our Health is Community

Kulus envisions health systems that return power to individuals and communities. We centre Indigenous leadership, cultural knowledge, and the rights of people to control their own data.

We respectfully acknowledge the Kwakwaka'wakw peoples — their traditional territories include the northern part of Vancouver Island and the adjacent mainland of British Columbia — and we honour their knowledge and stewardship.

About Kulus

Kulus draws inspiration from the younger brother of the thunderbird — a guardian of learning and community care. We work with Indigenous leaders to build respectful, community-led approaches to health — starting with diabetes and pre-diabetes.

Our approach is rooted in sovereignty, cultural continuity and practical supports for wellbeing.

  • Data Sovereignty — communities and individuals control their health information.
  • Holistic Health — integrating cultural practices and traditional foods alongside modern care.
  • Accessibility — practical supports that make health achievable at every age.

You Are the Owner, Not the Product

We imagine systems that respect agency: open standards, clear consent, and real options for communities to decide how data is used.

  • Open, secure and accessible standards for portability and control
  • Patients as data owners and customers — not products
  • Meaningful choice to share or to monetize data only with clear consent
  • Security and privacy that empower, not obscure
